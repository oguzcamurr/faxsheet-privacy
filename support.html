Common questions

Does Faxsheet send faxes? No. It creates the cover sheet PDF. Share it to your fax app or online fax service, or print it for a fax machine.

What does "Pages" mean? The total number of pages you are sending, including the cover sheet.

My message is cut off. The message shrinks to fit one page. If it has too many lines, Faxsheet warns you; shorten it or attach the details as a separate page.

I bought Pro on another device. Tap ••• → Restore Purchase.
