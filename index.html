Faxsheet — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026 · App: Faxsheet (iOS) · Developer: Oguz Camur · Contact: oguzcamuur@outlook.com

Summary

Faxsheet does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no network features of its own.

What Faxsheet stores

  • Your details and recent recipients. The sender details you enter and up to 12 recent recipients are saved in the app's private storage on your iPhone so you don't have to type them again.
  • Your current draft. The cover sheet you are working on is saved on your iPhone.

Deleting the app deletes this data. Like other app data, it can be included in your own iCloud or computer backups of your device.

Exporting and printing

When you share or print a cover sheet, iOS shows its share sheet or print dialog and the PDF goes where you choose (for example Files, Mail, a fax app or a printer). Faxsheet does not send faxes and does not receive a copy of anything you share.

Purchases

The optional Faxsheet Pro purchase is processed by Apple. The developer does not receive your payment details.

Confidentiality notice

The optional notice printed on the cover sheet is a courtesy message to the recipient. It does not make a fax transmission secure, and Faxsheet makes no claim about the security of the fax service or machine you use.

Children

Faxsheet is a business tool and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
